# Pakistan Test Question Bank Seed

The application seeds a broad, idempotent starter bank for Pakistani academic, admission and recruitment testing. Existing teacher questions are never overwritten and restart does not create duplicate question text.

2. Chemistry

4. English

6. Current Affairs

9. Mathematics
10. Islamiat

15. World History
16. Pedagogy & Education
17. Logical Reasoning
18. Analytical Reasoning
19. Quantitative Reasoning
20. Basic Statistics
21. Constitution & Civics

Every subject has at least **52 verified starter MCQs**. The original nine subjects also retain their curated core set, so they have more than 52. Every question includes four options, a correct-answer key, topic, tags, explanation, display order and verification state.

## Teacher paper builder

The teacher opens a subject folder to see its available questions. Selecting a checkbox saves that question in the examination immediately and removes it from the available list, preventing accidental duplicate selection. Examination marks are redistributed across selected questions while preserving the exact paper total.

## Maintenance note

The built-in bank is a safe starter/demo bank. Teachers should review wording against the exact syllabus and recruitment advertisement before publication. Time-sensitive Current Affairs items should be refreshed regularly; the seeded international-organisation questions intentionally favour stable facts.
